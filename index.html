Opening Range → EOD Direction Backtester

Tests the concept from these notes: does the direction of the first 15‑minute (and an optional custom N‑minute) opening window agree with the end-of-day direction? Loads any intraday OHLC CSV, computes per‑session signals, and reports conditional hit rates plus an agreement matrix.

Extension beyond the opening range, in OR sizes

Upside extension = (day high − OR high) / OR size. Downside extension = (OR low − day low) / OR size. Both are ≥ 0 by construction; together with 1 they sum to the day‑range / OR multiple.
